- **Data Center Wars**: NVIDIA and Intel are not just competitors—they’re **scaling faster**. NVIDIA’s H100 and A100 chips dominate AI workloads, while Intel’s hybrid architecture is gaining traction. AMD’s EPYC and Instinct lines are **lagging in performance** compared to these rivals. If the data center market slows (as it often does during economic downturns), AMD’s market share gains could reverse.  
- **Gaming GPUs**: While AMD’s Radeon RX series is strong, NVIDIA’s RTX 40 series has **dominated the market**. Even with 3D V-Cache, AMD’s GPUs are still **price-sensitive** and **lagging in ray tracing**. The “redefining the game” narrative is smoke and mirrors.  

The bull’s emphasis on “scalability” ignores the **infrastructure limitations**. AMD’s ability to scale depends on **chip manufacturing**—a sector where TSMC’s dominance is unshakable. If AMD can’t secure enough foundry capacity, its “platform play” could stall.
